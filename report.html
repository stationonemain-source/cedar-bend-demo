Report a problem

Potholes, streetlights, water leaks, stray animals, high grass, missed trash. Tell us where and we will get it to the right crew. You get a ticket number by email.

Emergency, or water gushing? Call 911 or the after-hours utility line, (512) 555-0149.

What is it?

A street address or the nearest cross streets.

So we can send the ticket number and tell you when it is done.

Required fields are marked with an asterisk. We do not share your details, and you can report anonymously by leaving the contact fields blank; you just won't get updates.
