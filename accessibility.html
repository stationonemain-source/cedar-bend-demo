Accessibility

This website is built to meet WCAG 2.1 Level AA, the standard adopted by the Department of Justice's ADA Title II rule for state and local governments.

What that means in practice

  • Every page works with a keyboard alone and with screen readers such as NVDA, JAWS and VoiceOver.
  • Text can be enlarged to 200% without losing content.
  • Colors meet the required contrast, and no information is carried by color alone.
  • Documents posted since launch are tagged PDFs with real titles, headings and image descriptions.
  • The site is re-tested every quarter, and after any major change.

Something not working for you?

Tell us. Email accessibility@cedarbendtx.gov or call (512) 555-0140. Say which page or document, and what happened. We will fix it or provide the information in another format within five business days.

Older documents

Some PDFs from before this site launched are not yet accessible. If you need one, ask and we will convert it, usually within two business days.
