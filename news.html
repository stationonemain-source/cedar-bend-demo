Pecan Street water main repair scheduled for Sept 22

September 18, 2026

Crews will replace a 6-inch main between 3rd and 5th on Tuesday, September 22, from 8 a.m. to about noon. Homes on Pecan, 4th and the 300 block of Elm will have low pressure or no water during the work. Run a cold tap for two minutes afterward if the water looks cloudy. Questions: (512) 555-0142.

Council adopts the 2026–27 budget; tax rate unchanged

September 15, 2026

The council adopted a $6.4 million budget on Tuesday and kept the property tax rate at $0.4821 per $100 of value. The budget adds one police officer and funds the Pecan Street water line. Adopted budget (PDF, 58 pages)

Fall bulk trash pickup

September 9, 2026

North of Main Street: Monday, October 12. South of Main: Tuesday, October 13. Set items at the curb the night before. No tires, paint or appliances with refrigerant.

Pool closes for the season Sept 7

September 2, 2026

Last swim is Labor Day. Thank you to the 14 lifeguards who worked this summer. Season passes for 2027 go on sale in April.
