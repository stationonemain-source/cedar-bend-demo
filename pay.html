Pay a water bill

One bill covers water, sewer and trash. Due on the 15th. A 10% late fee applies after the 25th.

Online, in about a minute

  1. Find your account number
    It is in the top right of your paper bill, eight digits.
  2. Open the payment portal
    You will leave this site for the City's payment processor. There is a $1.50 convenience fee for cards; bank drafts are free.
  3. Keep the confirmation email
    Payments post the next business day.
Pay online now

Opens the City's payment processor in a new page. (Demo: link disabled.)

Set up autopay

Bring a voided check to City Hall or download the bank draft form (PDF, 1 page). Drafts run on the 15th.

Other ways to pay

Trouble paying?

Call utility billing before the 25th and ask for a payment arrangement. One extension per year, no questions asked. (512) 555-0142
