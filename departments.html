Departments

One phone number reaches all of them: (512) 555-0140. Direct lines and what each office handles are below.

Water and utilities

Billing, new service, disconnects, leaks on the City side of the meter. Direct line (512) 555-0142. Pay a bill. To start service, bring a photo ID and a lease or deed; a $100 deposit is refunded after 12 on-time payments.

Public works

Streets, drainage, signs, trash and recycling. Trash is picked up Tuesdays; put the cart out by 7 a.m. Recycling runs every other Tuesday. Report a pothole or missed pickup.

Police

Emergency: 911. Non-emergency (512) 555-0150. Accident reports are available three business days after the incident at the front desk, 400 E. Main St.

Building and permits

Building permits, inspections, zoning questions and code compliance. Most residential permits (fences, sheds, water heaters) are issued the same day if the form is complete.

  • Residential building permit application (PDF, 2 pages)
  • Yard sale permit (free, online form)
  • Fee schedule 2026–27 (PDF, 3 pages)

Parks and recreation

Pavilion rentals at Cedar Bend Park are $40 for residents, $75 for non-residents, first come first served by phone. The pool is open Memorial Day to Labor Day.

City Secretary

Public information requests, elections, ordinances, council support. Under the Texas Public Information Act, most requests are answered within 10 business days. Email records@cedarbendtx.gov or use the records request form (online).
